# Plan: Stage 3, row POL: `polity.go` and `internal/polity`

**Status: written (2026-11-13), stage 1 of the work (requirements and plan, no code). Revised after the audit of 2026-11-13 (2 blocking, 9 non-blocking items; see Audit log at the end); not yet re-audited. Branch `s3-pol`, cut from `phase-3` (= `origin/main` f556398).**
Builds the polity module: jurisdictions as one tree over the regions, the membership of the regions, the deterministic queries (chain, covers, common, regions, crossing, between), the treasury account and pseudo-agent of each governed jurisdiction, the three combining modes for the rows a later reader keys by jurisdiction, and the neutrality of the module while no jurisdiction is governed. It has no tick position, no draw, no event, no slot and no checkpoint section of its own. It is off unless `Config.Modules.Polity` is present, and while it is off it leaves no trace (architecture.md 2.10.5).

Rule tags read when the plan was written: architecture=bdb8a4b7fa1ed8fd3b2e7aec, observability=f215753306fac0bc7607c4c4, testing=66af7645b01fef4e2796c367. The plan edits no spec and no rule file, so it does not quote a notes tag; whoever applies Phase 1 reads `doc/notes.md` in full and quotes its tag (AGENTS.md). One exception depends on Q2: if the owner confirms the `PolityConfig` rename, the sentence it adds to `spec-config.md` 3.7.1 is a spec edit (R1), and then the notes-tag rule binds that change.

Specs implemented: `spec-polity.md` (POL) all of it (FR-POL-001 to 035, NFR-POL-001 to 004); `spec-ledger.md` (LED) 3.1, 7.2 row `initial`, 9.3 (FR-LED-025, FR-LED-065); `spec-world.md` (WLD) 5.1 steps 5, 7, 8, 5.2 row 5a, 5.3 (FR-WLD-030, 031, 076); `spec-config.md` (CFG) 3.1, 6.2 (polity tables), 8.2 (FR-CFG-092); `spec-observability.md` (OBS) 4.3.3 item 4, 5.3 (`Jurisdictions`, `MoneyTreasury`), 7.3 row 1 (FR-OBS-041 with a governed jurisdiction); `spec-checkpoint.md` (CHK) 4.2 kind `5`, 4.2.2 (FR-CHK-048); `spec-integration.md` (INT) 6.2 (`Polity`, `TreasuryAgent`, class 1 and 3) and 3.4.3, 4.5.1 (a treasury ID in a call); `spec-reference-chain.md` (REF) 4.5 (`overlay/polity.toml`, `overlay/polity-governed.toml`), scenarios S112, S113.

## Scope

In:

- `polity.go` (root, one file for the module; architecture.md 2.6, AGENTS.md Layout): `JurisdictionID`, `NoJurisdiction`, `Polity` (the configuration type with `Jurisdictions`, `Members`), `Jurisdiction`, `Member`, `Crossing`, the value `Polity` is read through, `NewPolity`, `World.Polity`, `World.TreasuryAgent`, `validatePolity` and the two warnings, the module state (`*polity` in `World`), the opening of the treasury accounts and the pseudo-agents, the `StartMoney` transfer, the snapshot rows, the log summary. If Lizard (N-243) forces a split, a continuation file `polity_treasury.go` (POL) is added to architecture.md 2.6 and AGENTS.md Layout first, in the same change (the plan expects no split: no tick position).
- `internal/polity` (standard library and `internal/checked` only): the tables from `parent []int32` and `member []int32`, the chain, covers, common, covered lists, crossing, the edge-pair test of `Between` over primitive slices, and the three combiners of 6.1.2 (innermost, added, scaled with the one rounding step left to the owner through a parameter). No root type (architecture.md 2.9).
- `internal/ledger`: the row `initial` runs boundary to agent, estate or treasury (money); no other row changes (the `trade` and fiscal rows belong to FIS). `KindTreasury`, `sectorTreasury` and the sectoral audit already exist (Stage 1); the plan adds the tests that exercise them.
- Catalogue appends (table below), `config/mapping.go` (the tables `[modules.polity]`, `[modules.polity.jurisdiction.<id>]`, `[modules.polity.member]`), `doc.go` glossary, `internal/logkeys` constants, the overlays `testdata/chains/overlay/polity.toml` and `polity-governed.toml`, the golden `testdata/golden/polity/`, the scenarios S112 and S113.
- The world plumbing that is core: `firstHousehold` grows by the number of treasuries, the agent-ID range checks that mean "a pseudo-agent" (found by `grep firstHousehold`: 41 non-test lines in the root package at the time of the audit, most already generic; R5 reads each), the refusal of `Relocate` and of the calls that name an agent for a treasury ID, the restore that attaches and checks the treasury accounts, the audit.
- `otelobserver`: `economysim.money.supply` takes the sector `treasury` fed by `RegionRow.MoneyTreasury` (the sector value is already in `cells.go` and `adapter_test.go`; the feed is not).

Out, with the owner:

- Anything a treasury does: levies, tolls, tariffs, payouts, standing orders (FIS); the MON target kind `treasury` and `FlowTarget.Jurisdiction` (FIS; they stay in `notBuilt`); a policy actor per jurisdiction (PLC); who holds a title or a store (LND, STO, reserved); rights and controls keyed by jurisdiction (ORG). POL offers `Polity`, `TreasuryAgent` and the combiners and no more.
- A transfer of a region, a law that forbids a move across a border, a second treasury, a treasury that places orders (spec-polity.md 7.4, 17: not offered).
- The measures by jurisdiction and the `M_t` term of `spec-indicators.md` 8.2 (IND ask; a fold over `Regions(j)`). The preset and the regional identity are not built in this row. The treasury sector is only reported in the snapshot.
- The all-on set (`testdata/golden/all/`, `testdata/checkpoints/format-1-all.*`, `allLayers`): see D8 and Q1. This row does not touch them.
- The reserved MON/INT/ORG readers H3 to H14: read-only consumers of `Polity`; each is the row of the module that uses it.

## Minimal slice

The roadmap implies no slice; the spec has one natural cut. **Slice A** is the module with no governed jurisdiction: configuration, validation, hash, loader, the `Polity` value and its queries, the names table, the warnings, the summary, the combiners and the neutrality of FR-POL-019. It creates no account and no agent, shifts no ID, and moves no existing golden; the ungoverned overlay and the neutrality test (S112) belong to it. **Slice B** is the treasury: accounts, pseudo-agents, `StartMoney`, the snapshot and `WorldCreated` rows, restore checks, the module-alone golden. FIS, ORG, PLC and the reserved readers need Slice A for their keys, and FIS and PLC need Slice B (`Modules.Fiscal` needs a governed jurisdiction, CFG 7; `Modules.Policy` needs a jurisdiction). So the two are two pull requests (PR split below), A first (Q3).

## Decisions

D1. **One file, no event, no position.** `polity.go` holds the state struct, `validatePolity`, the construction calls, the snapshot rows and the checkpoint attach; there is no `tick.go` entry, no `notBuiltPositions` change, no `event.go` type, no `option.go` option, no `random.go` site (FR-POL-020, 021). The position table test and the event catalogue test are expected to be unchanged, and a phase exit checks it.
D2. **Hash id 19.** `Modules.Polity` is the 19th pointer of the `Modules` row (Settlements 11, Storage 12, Employment 13, Tenure 14, Credit 15, Risk 16, Infrastructure 17, Insurance 18, Polity 19, Fiscal 20; Employment is already 13 in `pairsLaterModules`). Its Go line is appended after `Employment` in `Modules`, in `canonical` and in `modulesOn`; its pair is written in `pairsLaterModules` after id 13 and before id 25 (the ascending-id rule of `pairsModules`). Jurisdictions are written sorted by ID, members by `RegionID`; `Label` is not written. The pair of a non-nil pointer is written even when empty (a module that is on and empty differs from one that is off).
D3. **No checkpoint tag.** The module adds no section (spec-polity.md 11; the tag list of CHK 4.2 has none and `notBuiltSections` does not list one). The treasury accounts are in the ledger section as kind `5`; the restore attaches them after the Outside accounts and rejects a ledger that lacks, duplicates or misplaces one (FR-POL-030), as `attachAccounts` does for the counterparties.
D4. **Account and ID order.** Treasury accounts open in `beginConstruction` after `w.external.openAccounts` and before any agent account, in ascending `JurisdictionID`; the treasury agent IDs are `regions + counterparties .. regions + counterparties + g - 1`; `firstHousehold = regions + counterparties + g`. `assignAgents` for the treasuries runs after the counterparties'. With the module off or with no jurisdiction governed `g` is 0 and nothing moves.
D5. **`StartMoney` is `initial` into the treasury**, in the construction bucket, in ascending `JurisdictionID`, before `createAgents`. The registry row widens to `setAE | setTreasury` for money only. No new reason, faucet or sink (FR-POL-018). The `BoundaryFlow` row at `Tick` -1 of the seat's region therefore counts it as `initial` money; the observer rebuild subtracts `WorldCreated.Jurisdictions[].Money` (D7).
D6. **The `Polity` value mirrors `Graph`.** A struct of slices and integers built by `internal/polity` tables; `World.Polity()` returns a deep copy built on each call (class 3 of INT 6.2), the empty `Polity` with the module off, and is callable on a failed world. The scalar methods allocate nothing (NFR-POL-001; `AllocsPerRun` test).
D7. **Observability fields.** `Names.Jurisdictions []string` (nil with the module off), `WorldCreated.Jurisdictions []JurisdictionRow`, `Snapshot.Jurisdictions []JurisdictionRow`, `RegionRow.MoneyTreasury Money`, `"polity"` in `Snapshot.Modules` and in the `modules` list of the summary, `jurisdictions` and `governed` in the summary. Each appended field is a count, an amount, a bool, a constant or a slice, never a typed ID, so the off rendering is unchanged (architecture.md 2.10.5). `JurisdictionRow` carries `Jurisdiction` and `Agent` as the typed IDs the spec fixes; the rule "never a typed ID" binds fields appended to an *existing* event or row, and `WorldCreated.Jurisdictions` and `Snapshot.Jurisdictions` are new slices (zero rows when off, omitted from the rendering); the reading is checked in Phase 1 (item 1.7) against the text of architecture.md 2.10.5 and FR-OBS-091 and the golden renderer, before any code, and not left to Phase 6 (R4). Whether the rows carry typed IDs at all is Q9, still open with the owner; the plan does not decide it, and Phase 6 builds `JurisdictionRow` only after Q9 is answered (plain `int` fields and typed accessors are the fallback).
D8. **Existing goldens do not move.** `testdata/golden/off/` and the pinned hash stay unchanged (N-217). A module-alone golden `testdata/golden/polity/` is new. `golden/all/` and the all-on checkpoint fixture are not touched by this row: `allLayers` is not appended (Q1). The ungoverned overlay is proved neutral by comparison (S112), not by a second golden.
D9. **Rule files.** architecture.md 2.6 and AGENTS.md Layout already list `polity.go` and `internal/polity/`; observability-and-logging.md 3.3.2 already has the keys `jurisdictions` and `governed` and 5.4.1 the sector `treasury`. So no rule file changes and no tag is replaced; Phase 1 verifies each by text search and records that. If one is found missing, the edit and a fresh tag belong to the same change.
D10. **Registry and catalogue rows.** The entries `Polity` (line 64), `Jurisdiction`, `Member` (lines 63-64) and `Modules.Polity` (line 43) leave `notBuilt` in `registry_test.go`; `Modules.Polity` joins `exemptFields` (a single-line map, line 78) like the other pointers; `Polity` joins `scopeAlias` (line 94, today `Subsistence` only) only if R1 stands (Q2). The entry `"WorldCreated.Jurisdictions"` of `notBuiltFields` in `event_test.go` (line 113, "polity module, POL 10.7") is deleted in the same change that appends the field (Phase 6). The nine rows of the parameter-owner table already exist in `doc/specs/README.md`.
D11. **Sentinels are root sentinels** matched with `errors.Is` (N-075): new `ErrUnknownJurisdiction`, `ErrJurisdictionCycle`, `ErrSeatNotCovered`, `ErrSeatOutsideNode`, `ErrStartWithoutSeat`; reused `ErrInvalidID`, `ErrDuplicateID`, `ErrTooMany`, `ErrOutOfBounds` (also a label above `MaxLabelBytes`, spec 9.1), `ErrUnknownRegion`. POL uses no `ErrModuleDependency` (it needs no module). `ErrNotRelocatable` exists. `ErrSeatOutsideNode` arises only with the external module on.
D12. **Coverage row.** `internal/polity` gets the row `internal/polity 90` in `scripts/ci/coverage-thresholds.txt` (its header: the checker fails a package that is not listed), added in Phase 2 with the package. `import_guard_test.go` is generic and has no per-package list, so nothing is added there; the package is checked by the guard's existing rule for `internal/`.

### Phase 2: `internal/polity` (pure tables and combiners)

Goal: the definitions of spec-polity.md 4.1 and 6.1.2 as functions over primitive slices, proved against the model and the worked examples.

Files: `internal/polity/polity.go`, `doc.go`, `polity_test.go`, `fuzz_test.go`, `scripts/ci/coverage-thresholds.txt` (the new row; `scripts/ci/selftest.sh` run). Sketch of the API (made final here): `Build(parent []int32, member []int32, maxDepth int) (Tables, error)` (the error is a cycle or a depth overflow, mapped at the root); `Tables.Chain(r) []int32`, `Covers`, `Common`, `Regions(j)`, `Children(j)`, `Depth(j)`, `Crossing(a, b) (exit, enter []int32)`, `EdgeBetween(fromRegion, toRegion, a, b int32) bool`; `Innermost(rows []Row, chain []int32) (value int64, ok bool)`, `Added(...)`, `Scaled(base int64, factorsBP []int64) (int64, bool)`: the product of `base` and every factor in basis points divided by `10 000` to the power of the number of factors, with one rounding step. `internal/checked` has no 128-bit product helper to expose; it has `Mul`, `MulDiv`, `MulDivDiv(a, b, c, d)` (one 128-bit intermediate, one division by `c*d`), the floor, ceil and half-even forms, `MulAddDivSat`, `Cmp3` and `Acc.AddMul`/`DivHalfEven` (`acc.go`). The single rounding step is `Acc` (`AddMul` for the products, `DivHalfEven` once) or `MulDivDiv` for two factors; Phase 2 picks the one that keeps a chain of up to `MaxPolityDepth` factors exact, shared with the other modules (N-019), and states the choice in the package comment. The rounding mode (half even) is the spec's; if spec 6.1.2 leaves it to the owner of the reader, the parameter returns as a `Round` argument with the same signature, and that is recorded in the PR.

Tests (unit; `Requirement:` lines as shown): `TestBuild_Chains` (example A of 12.1: the five chains) FR-POL-006, 007. `TestBuild_Common` (all 25 ordered pairs of example A, border depth) FR-POL-008. `TestBuild_Regions` (covered lists include descendants' members) FR-POL-009. `TestCrossing_ExampleA` and `TestCrossing_ExampleB` (the tables of 12.1 and 12.2 with the arithmetic in the comment) FR-POL-010, 033. `TestCrossing_Reversal` (property over 200 random forests with a fixed seed per case, the failing seed in the message: `Crossing(b, a)` is the reversal with the lists swapped; Exit and Enter disjoint; a region with itself empty) FR-POL-010. `TestBuild_Rejects` (a cycle, a self-parent, depth `MaxPolityDepth`, a chain of exactly `MaxPolityDepth` entries builds) FR-POL-003, 004. `TestBuild_BoundSize` (1 000 jurisdictions, 1 000 regions, a deepest chain of 8 entries, against a second formulation that climbs to the lowest common ancestor, the model of `doc/specs/models/pol/chain.py` re-expressed in Go) FR-POL-004. `TestCombine_ExampleA` (the three rows of the 12.1 table: 200/300/200/350/300; 90/80/90/80/80; 900/720/900/720/720) FR-POL-023. `TestScaled_SingleRounding` (a case where two roundings differ from one; order of the chain does not change the result) FR-POL-023, Guard. `TestQueries_OutOfRange` (every query with -1, J, a huge ID: zero result, no panic) FR-POL-012. `FuzzBuild` (random parent and member arrays: never panics, an accepted forest agrees with the model, a cyclic one errors).

## Risks and open items

- **R1. Two things are called `Polity`.** Spec 8 names the configuration scope `Polity` and 4.2.1 names the query value `Polity`. Both cannot be root types. Resolved here by `PolityConfig` for the configuration, with the `scopeAlias` row in `registry_test.go` (the `Subsistence` precedent). The owner may prefer the opposite; it is a rename before any code (Q2, open). `spec-config.md` 1775 records `SubsistenceConfig` and "`Tenure` resolves to `TenureConfig`" as precedent, but the spec has no such sentence for Polity and uses `Polity` for both the scope and the query value (spec-polity.md 4.2.1, 8; spec-config.md 347-349; FR-CFG-092 says `Config.Modules.Polity`). If the rename stands, the sentence in `spec-config.md` 3.7.1 is a spec edit, so the notes-tag rule applies to that change; the plan does not make it before Q2 is answered.
- **R2. The shift of household IDs.** A seat shifts every household ID by `g` (POL-A1, N-117, N-124, N-162). It is stated, tested as a reported shift and not hidden; a paired comparison across a governed and an ungoverned world is not valid and the golden of PR B is a new file, not a diff of `five`.
- **R3. The adapter.** `otelobserver` decodes `WorldCreated` and the snapshot. If its tests compare field lists by reflection, the appended fields change a test fixture (`testdata/all_events.txt`, `catalogue_copy.md`, as the employment PR touched). The phase budgets one small edit there; not a behaviour change.
- **R4. "Never a typed ID" for an appended field (architecture.md 2.10.5).** `JurisdictionRow` carries `Jurisdiction` and `Agent` typed IDs, but only inside two new slices that are empty with the module off or with no governed jurisdiction, so a world without a treasury renders as before. The reading is checked in Phase 1 (item 1.7), before Phase 4, so `event.go` and `snapshot.go` are not reworked after PR A. Whether typed IDs are accepted is Q9 (open); if the owner or the check says otherwise, the rows carry `int` indices and the typed names move to methods.
- **R5. `firstHousehold` consumers.** About forty sites (41 non-test lines in the root package) use `firstHousehold`; most test `id < firstHousehold`. Most are generic (a pseudo-agent), a few may assume "estate or counterparty". Phase 5 reads each, and the tests of `TestPolity_TreasuryRefusals` pin the behaviour of `Direct`, `Relocate`, `RemoveAgent` and `AddAgent` for a treasury ID (INT 3.4.3, 4.5.1).
- **R6. The merge order against the other Stage 3 rows.** FIS, ORG, PLC read POL, and the ledger registry, `construct.go` and the all-on set are shared (the conflict forecast). POL should merge first among the rows that touch the ledger registry.
- **R7. Unmeasured costs.** The operation counts of 4.4.2 are counts, not timings (N-015). The plan asserts structure, not speed. The bounds `MaxJurisdictions` 1 000 and `MaxPolityDepth` 8 are guesses (spec open question 7).
- **R8. Run time of S112 and S113.** Ten seeds of 200 ticks on `five`, twice, plus the golden and the fork, add to gate F (about 45 minutes on the 32-core host). `five` ticks are cheap relative to the all-on pack; the cost is measured on the first run and recorded in the PR, and the seed count drops to `scenarioSeedCount()` under `-race`.
- **R9. Spec text owed.** `spec-polity.md` 18 says "the rule files, at the first code (owed rows, with a fresh tag each)" for architecture.md 2.6 and 3.3 and the two observability rows. 2.6 and the observability rows are present already; architecture.md 3.3 (`JurisdictionID` among the typed IDs) is checked in Phase 1.2 and edited with a fresh tag if missing.
