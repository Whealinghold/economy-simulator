- **Events:** `MigrationStarted` (per agent, per departure) and `MigrationReviewed` (an aggregate per region, bounded by the regions), plain data, no map, constructed only under `w.obs.supplied`, after the transition (observability-and-logging.md 4.1, 4.5; D495). The arrival is `AgentRelocated` of plan 3 with the cause `migration`.
- **Warnings:** at construction the seven of MIG 12.3 with `rule` and `path`, and `pairs` (plan 13's `Warning.Count`) for `migration_provision` and `migration_far`; `migration_flow_window` carries no value (E5, O3); in the tick two, bounded per region: "migration blocked" and "arrivals held", each with an event counterpart (`MigrationReviewed`, rows 16 and 17 of spec-observability.md 7.3). Never per agent.
- **Debug:** "migration started", "migration declined", "arrival held", guarded by `Enabled`. No `Info` in the tick, no `Error`.
- **Metrics:** none defined in the core; the three instruments and the attribute `economysim.limit` are fed by these events (rules 5.3.1). `economysim.migration.started` adds at most `MaxRegions` squared series (20 for the reference world); `economysim.limit` adds 12 values.
- **Non-interference:** the draws of D11 do not depend on an observer; a run with and without observer and logger gives identical results (Phase 7 test).
