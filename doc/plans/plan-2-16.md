## Decisions

- **D520.** The three positions are WLD's `P1.8` (the walk, owner EXT, `line`), `P3.5` (the counterparties' orders, after `P3.4` and `P3.4a`) and `P4.2` (the sweep, after `P4.1` and `P4.1a`, before `P5.1`). EXT 5's "phase 1, 3, 4" is read as these positions; where the wordings differ the world spec wins. Source: spec-world.md 6 (rows P1.8, P3.5, P4.2), 6.12; EXT 5.1 to 5.3.
- **D521.** `Modules.External *External` is declared in `config.go`; its hash field id is 7 (the position in the `Modules` row, written as an explicit id in `hash.go`, inserted between ids 6 and 8 in ascending id order, so the Go declaration order, which is the build order, changes nothing), its checkpoint tag is 18, its events follow OBS 4.1.7. The off test: `Modules.External == nil` leaves `TestHashConfig_ReferencePinned` and `testdata/golden/off/` as they are. Source: CFG 3.1, 8.2.1; CHK 4.2; architecture.md 2.10.5.
- **D522.** The six reasons are constants of `internal/ledger`, appended in build order after the rows of plans 5 to 15 (plan 5 D311), displayed in the order of LED 7.2 by the rank column (`outside_budget`, `outside_unspent`, `outside_receipts`, `outside_offer`, `outside_unsold`, `outside_purchases`); account creation is in group 3 of plan 5's function; they are declared reasons by the mechanism of plan 5 (D311: declared-ness is a bit of the registry row, a ledger has them only when `Config.Declared` names them, no `firstDeclared` threshold is touched). They fill group 3 of that function: after the flow accounts of plan 6 and before `want_consumption`; their `BoundaryFlow` rows are the block between the money flows and the declared module rows. With the module off the walked fields of the ledger (`off [NumReasons][2]int` included) do not change. The numbers are internal and never saved (N-225); the root names the six in `ledger.go` (`reasonNames`). The `trade` row gains the Outside kind on both sides. Source: LED 7.2, 7.2.2; FR-LED-057, 058; N-225.
- **D523.** Draw D2 gets its own site, `siteOutsideWalk = 0x46AE095E114979AB` (first eight bytes, big-endian, of SHA-256 of `economy-sim/site/siteOutsideWalk`, the rule of WLD 7.1.4; checked against `siteWork` with the same command), keyed by `(tick, id = node RegionID, sub = CommodityID)`: four words, like D7. This is the owner's answer to ADR 0008 open point 3 for D2 only (a line is named by two IDs that a configuration change does not shift, where a row index would shift when a line is added before it); D13 stays open. It is a proposal until Phase 1 writes it into WLD 7.1.4 and 7.3. Source: ADR 0008 open point 3; N-214; EXT 5.1.1.
- **D524.** The walk takes exactly one word per walking line per tick, `drawRange(-WalkStepBP, WalkStepBP)` of `random.go` after `keyedPair(siteOutsideWalk, tick, node, commodity)`; the order of the lines (ascending node `RegionID`, `CommodityID`) is then not a result, only the order of the `Debug` lines. No draw at construction (FR-EXT-044). Source: EXT 6.4.1; WLD 7.2.1; ADR 0008.
- **D525.** The pseudo-agents are the estates, then the counterparties: `World.firstHousehold` (= regions + counterparties) replaces `len(w.cfg.Regions)` as "the first household agent" at the sites that Stage 1 spells that way (about 13: the loops and boundaries of `phase_outcomes.go`, `phase_exit.go`, `phase_start.go`, `phase_production.go`, `phase_trade.go`, `construct.go`, and the sizing in `allocate`; Phase 5 lists them by grep). With the module off it equals `len(Regions)`. Source: WLD 5.3; FR-EXT-011; FR-AGT-032; C21.
- **D526.** An outside node is marked by `World.outside []bool` (one per region, built from `cfg.Modules.External.Nodes`, all false when off) read through `isOutsideNode(RegionID)`. A node keeps its estate and its dense market slots but has no market in effect: it is skipped by `p41Clear`, by `MarketCleared`, by the snapshot rows of markets and roles (OBS 5.3.4), by the checkpoint markets section, by the cost anchors, the entry step, the spawner and every loop WLD 6.1.11 names. Its `RegionRow` has `Outside` true and the rest zero. Source: WLD 6.1.11; EXT 3.1.2; OBS 5.3.
- **D527.** The mid is `checked.MulDivHalfEven(Base*S, F*Shock, 10^12)` with `F = clamp(MulDivHalfEven(F_trend, F_walk, 10 000), FloorBP, CeilingBP)` and `F_trend = max(0, 10 000 + checked.MulDiv(TrendBP, t, TicksPerYear))`: `Base*S` is at most 10^14 and `F*Shock` at most 10^11, so the single rounding of EXT 6.1 is kept with the existing 128-bit helper and no new one. Source: EXT 6.1; `internal/checked`; architecture.md 3.5, N-019.
- **D528.** The quotes are `MulDivCeil` (ask), `MulDivFloor` (bid), the gate scale with the same rounding, then `min(MaxPrice)`; a bid below 1 places nothing (`CutPrice`). The wanted units are `(Capacity*Avail + carry) / 10 000` with the remainder as the new carry. The cap arithmetic: offered = `min(wanted, unitsLeft, valueLeft / limit)`, the difference is `CutCap`; a side ends at P4.2 when `unitsLeft == 0` or `valueLeft < limit`. Source: EXT 6.6, 7.1, 7.3.
- **D529.** Line state lives in `external` as flat slices indexed by line and side index, in the canonical order of the configuration, no map: drift, shock, and per side row carry, units, list value, ended. The key of a side row (per line side, or per line side and gate) is the ruling R1 of Phase 1; the plan is written for the per-gate reading, which is the one OBS 5.3.9 already has, and only `sideIndex` and the loops of P3.5 and P4.2 change under the other. Source: EXT 3.3, 7.1, 16; CHK 4.2 tag 18; OBS 5.3.9.
- **D530.** A counterparty's order uses the estate's path: `orderFacts{AgentKnown: true, SideAllowed: side matches, CommodityKnown: true, Tradable: true, Tally: &w.receipts}` through `book.admit`, then `emitPlaced` and `markets.record(mi, intentRec{})`; a refusal goes to `emitRejected` and is a programmer error in a validated world. The top-up precedes admission and uses `Transfer(boundary, cp, limit*offered, outside_budget)` or `Move(boundary, cp, c, offered, outside_offer)`, which creates the lot in the current tick at full durability (`creationKey`). Source: EXT 4.3, 5.2; FR-EXT-013, 014.
- **D531.** The sweep at P4.2 moves, per counterparty in agent ID order, money then goods in commodity order, whole lots, under `outside_unspent` / `outside_receipts` and `outside_unsold` / `outside_purchases` by the table of EXT 5.3.1; a quantity of 0 is not moved. Traded units and value are the amounts of the sweep, so the events need no trade tally. Source: EXT 5.3, 8.2.
- **D532.** The module has no `Option`, no extension interface and no `Stateful`. Nothing in `external.go` reads a balance, a price of the realm or an aggregate (FR-EXT-023, EXT 11.3): a test greps the file for `AgentView`, `Snapshot`, `Balance` and `markets.reports`. Source: EXT 9, 11.3; FR-EXT-023, 033.
- **D533.** `OutsideEnded.Cause` reuses the type `Cause` with two new constants, `CauseUnits = "units"` and `CauseValue = "value"`; it also declares the `Reason` constants `ReasonOutsideNode = "outside_node"` (REG 6.1.3 row 5a, returned with the sentinel `ErrOutsideNode`; plan 3 defers it here) and `ReasonOutsideDestination = "outside_destination"` (TRN 5.4 row 6a; plan 12 defers it here), appended after the reasons of plans 12 and 15; `OutsideTraded.Side` is the existing `Side` (`Bid` for a buy side, `Ask` for a sell side). Source: EXT 15.1; event.go.
- **D534.** Validation lives in `validateExternal(c *Config, r *report)` and `externalWarnings(c *Config, add)`, called by one line each from `validate.go` after the rules of the modules it depends on; the gates are derived from `Config.Edges` directly (sorted), not from a `Graph`. `ErrModuleDependency` when `Config.Edges` is empty. A flow ID equal to one of the six names is `ErrReasonCollision`, checked here against the money-flow IDs because the registry of plan 6 cannot know a later module's reasons. Source: EXT 14; FR-EXT-017, 042.
- **D535.** The shock slot is `external` state with an internal form `applyShocks(changes []outsideShock) (events []OutsideShocked)` that assumes validated input and changes nothing when a value is unchanged; the public call, its error order and its sentinels are plan 17's. Source: EXT 6.5; INT 5.1.3; FR-EXT-027, 037.
- **D536.** Import is a source for the structural pass and for V1 by one predicate, `importSourced(c *Config, commodity string) bool` (a sell side present, at least one gate for it, a window not 0 in every season), added to the `raw_without_source` expression of `warnings` in `validate.go`, to the producibility worklist of `structure`, and to `Realm.Sourced` in `viability_check.go`, each as an or-term. Source: EXT 10.1; CFG 7.5.
- **D537.** Logging: `Info` only in the construction summary (adds `nodes`, `counterparties`, and `external` in `modules`); `Warn` "outside side ended" once per side (`node`, `commodity`, `side`, `cause`); `Debug` "outside side" per side per tick and "outside drift" per walking line, each behind `Enabled`; constants `Node`, `Value`, `Nodes`, `Counterparties`, `Drift` join `internal/logkeys` as constants only; `logkeys_test.go` is not edited (plan 2); the rows are already in the table of observability-and-logging.md 3.3.2). Source: EXT 15.2; OBS 7.3 row 13.
- **D538.** The overlay `testdata/chains/overlay/external.toml` adds one node region to pack `one` (after plan 8's rebalance), two edges to `north`, one sell line of a raw input and one buy line of the staple, with capacities of about a tenth of the region's volume; it carries no season table (pack `one` has no seasons), so the window and season tests build their own worlds in Go. The module-alone golden is `testdata/golden/external/{one.txt,events-one.txt,one.positions.txt}`. Source: REF 4.5 (`overlay/external.toml`); testing.md 7.1.1.
- **D539.** The scenarios: S52, S53, S54 and S55 assert their exact checks and report their measures; S51 only reports (its bands are STY's, `t03.pull_bp`; N-113). S52 sets the closed edge through plan 3's internal edge-status setter (plan 17 switches that call to the public `SetEdgeStatuses`); S55 is race-aware as plan 1 defines (seed count by `raceEnabled`). Source: spec-stability.md 8 (S51 to S55); REF 7.1.5, FR-REF-022, 023; plan-2-1.
