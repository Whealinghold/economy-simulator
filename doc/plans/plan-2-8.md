Before any simulation a candidate must satisfy, over the groups that have an `IncomeBP`: the smallest at least 8 000 and the largest at most 1.5 times the smallest. These two figures are this plan's screening values; the owner confirms or replaces them (Risks, item 2). Groups without an `IncomeBP` (D372: no labour share, as in `famine`, or a recipe shared by several roles, flagged ambiguous) are outside the gate and listed with the reason; a pack with none of them has no gate and the run alone judges it. A pack that fails the gate is rejected without a run: the solver is exact and cheap, the run is not. Roles that cannot be fixed (the thin gatherer groups of `five`, REF 16 question 9) are listed with the reason and do not block. The gate is a test, so a pack edit that breaks it is seen (D370).

- **D372 (the report column, and why it is not a new census).** `IncomeBP(g) = w(rho) * labour(g) * 10^4 / (H(g) * U)` rounded half to even, with `labour(g)` the labour term of the group's recipes at the flows of lambda 1 (`sum of d(r) * x(r)`, VIA 5.2; not the larger term and not multiplied by `lambda*`, because the wage already carries `lambda*`: `w` is about `lambda* * U`), `w` the labour dual in minor units per working tick, `H(g)` the configured households and `U` the upkeep cost per household-tick in minor units at the solved prices. Check on `one`: cook `2512 * 75 / (136 * 999)` is about 138%, grower `2512 * 47.56 / (253 * 999)` about 47%. Identity: the households-weighted mean over all groups is `w * LabourShareBP / U`, about 10 000. Absent (flagged) when the labour share is absent (a tight capacity row, FR-VIA-012) and for a row flagged ambiguous (a recipe on the lists of several roles). It is computed in `viability_report.go` from values the solver already holds; overflow is checked (`internal/checked`). It is a **report field** (an append to `CensusRow`); the census, the tables and the hash do not read it. Source: N-314 (a), the owner's request to add income per household per role to the report.
