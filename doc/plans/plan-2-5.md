## Scope

**Builds.** `demand.go` (the module file, in the order of 2.10.1: state, `validateDemand`, the per-position methods P3.6 and P6.2, the filler of the want group of `AgentView`, the checker of the `TradePlan`, the items for the tradable set, the removal step, the checkpoint encoder and decoder, the term of the reference programme); `internal/household` additions (the pure rules of DEM 4.2 to 4.7); `internal/steady` two-phase start; the `tradableItems` fifth fan-in; `Modules.Demand` and its types; the loader keys of `config/mapping.go`; the ledger reason `want_consumption`; `WantShortfall`; the overlay, the module-alone golden and the scenarios S23, S24, S25.
**Public identifiers.** `Demand`, `Want`, `WantItem`, `Taste`, `RegionTaste`, `Modules.Demand`, `WantID`, `Names.Wants`, `DefaultWant`, `DefaultWantItem`, `DefaultTaste`, `DefaultRegionTaste`, `ErrUnknownWant`, `ErrWantItems`, `ErrWantDurable`, `WantShortfall`, `ReasonWantConsumption`, the reasons `choked`, `over_budget`, `no_budget`, `no_stock`, `Intent.WantUnits`, `TradePlan.WantFunded` and `WantFunding`, `AgentView.Wants` and `WantView`, `Report.SurplusWantsBP`.
**Leaves out.** Tier-1 rationing (N-052, rejected in DEM 17). Durable wants (`ErrWantDurable`, DEM open question 4). Growth of wants over time (DEM 7.3 item 4). The metric `economysim.want.unfunded` and the `Warn` aggregation to OTel: the metric is fed by the event and built by plan 22 (`otelobserver`). The money a non-producing class needs (MON, EXT, EMP, LND, FIS; DEM 7.3). The census of `one-demand`, the rebalance and the pack's stability: plan 8 (D315, D317). The effective reference price per region: plan 3 (`Config.refPrice`). The indicators `Cons` and `WCons`: plan 18. Statements about `five` and `second` with wants: plan 24.
