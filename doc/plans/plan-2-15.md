## Decisions

- **D500** Three root files if the Lizard split is made, else `contract.go` alone (E4): `contract.go` owns the state, the validator, the view filler, the plan checker, the removal step, the refusal, the snapshot rows and the checkpoint codec; `contract_round.go` would hold admission, keys and formation; `contract_settle.go` settlement and removal. `World` gains `contracts *contracts`, nil unless `Modules.Merchants` is present. Source: architecture 2.6, 2.10.1, N-243; spec-merchants 23.
- **D501** `internal/merchant` imports `internal/checked` and `internal/ordering` (`Weyl32` exists, with its evenness test). It does not import `internal/household`: the strategy computes the reserve with `household.ReserveMoney` itself. Source: architecture 2.9; spec 23 (names `ordering`) against NFR-MER-003 (names `household`): the spec contradicts itself, Phase 1 E1.
- **D502** The planned trip enters the default's tiers through one new public method, `DefaultStrategy.TradeWith(v, rng, p, trip []TripInput)`, with `TripInput{Commodity CommodityID; Quantity Quantity; Purpose Purpose}`; `Trade` is `TradeWith(..., nil)`. With a non-nil `trip` the planned recipe is none, `Job_c` is the sum of the entries for the commodity (`StartsIn` is ignored), `OutTgt_c` is 0 and the intent's `Purpose` is the entry's. The merchant then lowers `Limit` of the bids for the cargo and the haul units in the returned plan (FR-MER-016). Source: spec 3.3 and 3.4.2 describe the effect and name no seam; it is proposed, reversible, and Phase 1 E1 asks the owners to confirm it.
- **D503** The view gains `HasMerchant bool` and `Merchant MerchantView` (the shape of `HasDirective`/`Directive`): `Params` (the block by value and `MerchantFeeShare`), `Modes []MerchantMode` in mode order, `Goods []MerchantGood` in the tradable set's order (`Mass`, `Bulk`, `ShelfLife`, `Durability`, `OldestAge`). `Outbound` is plan 12's group on the view of every agent (TRN 9.1, FR-TRN-049), not a field of `MerchantView`. Source: spec 4.1; the placement of `Outbound` differs between MER 4.1 and AGT 6.4.1 (inside the group) and TRN 9.1 (own group): the plan follows TRN, section 9 item 4.
- **D504** `TradePlan` gains `Offers []DeliveryOffer`, `Takes []DeliveryTake`, `Notes PlanNotes`; `viewScratch.tradePlan()` empties all three. The plan checker of the module (`checkTradePlan` fan-in) adds `Notes` into a per-region tally and copies the agent's offers and takes, with the plan index and the plan's `len(Shipments)`, into the round's scratch. `PlanNotes` is declared in `contract.go` (not `strategy.go`). Source: spec 9.2, 15.1.2; architecture 2.10.4.
- **D505** Draw D6 gets the site `siteContract = 0xF0FF7DEE8E50504B` (the first eight bytes of SHA-256 of `economy-sim/site/siteContract`), keyed by `keyedPair(siteContract, tick, agent, sub)`, `sub` = `2*i` for the agent's `i`-th submitted offer and `2*i+1` for its `i`-th take (plan index, not admission index); the key is that stream's first word. One word per admitted entity, none otherwise, as FR-MER-033 counts. Source: spec-world 7.1.4 ("the id the owner chooses"), 7.3 D6, N-214, ADR 0008.
- **D506** The round is `internal/merchant.Match` over `Offer{Ref, Limit, Qty, Key}` and `Take{Ref, Limit, Qty, Key}` slices the root converts inline: offers by (`Limit` asc, `Key`, `Ref`), takes by (`Limit` desc, `Key`, `Ref`), the first take with `Limit >= offer.Limit` and remaining `>=` the offer's quantity, the price `halfEven((a+b)/2)`. It never calls `Clearing`. Source: spec 9.4, ADR 0005.
- **D507** `escrow_in`, `escrow_out`, `escrow_refund` are declared rows of `internal/ledger` appended to the `Reason` list before `NumReasons` (after the rows earlier plans declared), money only, internal, `Agent -> Escrow`, `Escrow -> Agent|Estate`, `Escrow -> Estate`; appended before `NumReasons` they must not grow `Ledger.off` (the per-position state hash walks it, so `testdata/golden/off/*.positions.txt` would move): use the mechanism of plan 5; the root names them `ReasonEscrowIn/Out/Refund` and declares them when `Modules.Merchants` is present. The Escrow account for a contract is the lowest-ID open `KindEscrow` account of the destination region that no open contract names, else a new one (a scan of the root's ascending `escrowAccts` slice, rebuilt at restore from the ledger). Source: spec 9.6, spec-ledger 7.2.2, FR-LED-060, 061.
- **D508** New exported constants in `event.go`: reasons `ReasonContractsOff`, `ReasonDuplicateTake`, `ReasonTooManyContracts`, `ReasonPayerFunds`, `ReasonNoTaker`, `ReasonOpenContract`; the type `SettleCause` with `SettledDelivered`, `SettledShort`, `SettledPayerRemoved`, `SettledDelivererRemoved`; the type `Decision` (a string, distinct from `Reason`, so `no_rumour` here does not repeat plan 14's `ReasonNoRumour` in `TestConstants_Values`) with the eleven decision constants of 5.1.2, `no_rumour` among them (spec-merchants 13 and 23: exported constants of the root package, the values of `economysim.decision`). Existing reasons are reused (`unknown_commodity`, `not_tradable`, `invalid_price`, `invalid_quantity`, `insufficient_stock`, `insufficient_funds`, `overflow`), and plan 12's for transport refusals. Source: spec 9.3, 9.5, 15.1.
- **D509** Contracts are a slice in ascending ID order (IDs are issued in formation order); settlement walks it once, looks the shipment up in the retained arrival record of P1.3 (`arrived []arrival{shipment, lines{shipped, delivered, diverted, spoiled}}`, sorted by shipment ID, held until P1.4; `Lost` is 0 until RSK) with a binary search, and compacts the settled out. Source: spec 9.7, 16.
- **D510** The removal step 2a sits in `removeAgent` before the money moves (architecture 2.10.4 slot): for each open contract of the removed agent as deliverer, `escrow_refund` the whole escrow to the payer (to the estate of the contract's destination region `r` if the payer is gone; spec-ledger line 500 says the same, spec-merchants 9.7 words it as the payer's region: owed edit E6), end it, emit `ContractSettled` with cause `deliverer_removed` and `Region` the deliverer's account region; a payer's contract is left. Source: spec 9.8.2, FR-MER-041, 042; agents 3.6 step 2a.
- **D511** `open_contract` is added to the `relocationRefusal` fan-in (`contract.go` function `contractRefusal`), open while the agent is payer or deliverer of an open contract, with the contract IDs as instance keys, and ignoring only the contract named in `skip` (the one formed on the arriving shipment); plan 12's `World.arrivalSkips(shipment, dst)` is the seam this plan fills with that contract key. The exact registry shape is plan 3's (section 9 item 2). Source: spec 9.8.1, regions 6.5.2, world C16, FR-MER-043.
- **D512** The welfare window of 12.3 (`T = max(WelfareWindow, WindowCycles * max(longest Duration, Kmax))` for a role with a block) is **not built by this plan** unless Phase 1 E5 records WEL adopting it; if adopted it is a ten-line change to `effectiveWindow` in `validate.go` guarded by `r.Merchant != nil`, tested by `TestEffectiveWindow_MerchantTrip` (N-099). Source: spec 12.3 ("WEL's to adopt").
- **D513** `MerchantsPlanned` is gated by a role having the block (`w.merchantRoles > 0`), not by the module, and is emitted at P3.3a (a `line` position owned by MER) even with `Modules.Merchants` absent; the other three events are gated by `w.contracts != nil`. Source: spec 15.1.1, world 6 P3.3a, FR-MER-055.
- **D514** The module-alone golden world is `two` + `overlay/trade-two.toml` (plan 3) + `overlay/transport-two.toml` (plan 12) + `overlay/information-two.toml` (plan 13) + `overlay/merchants.toml` (the `two` variant; `[modules.merchants]`, a merchant role and a consignee role; S70's `three` needs `merchants-three.toml`; there is no `five` variant, D516), seed 1, 400 ticks (REF 8.2, D257), in `testdata/golden/merchants/`: "alone" means the module and the modules it depends on (ErrModuleDependency). Source: testing 7.1.1, spec 14.1, MER-S6.
- **D515** Hash: `Modules.Merchants` is field 10 (spec-config 8.2.1); `Merchants.MaxOpen` is 1; `Role.MerchantFeeShare` is `Role` id 21 and `Role.Merchant` is 22 (after `Informed` 19 in plan 13 and `Fixed` 20 in plan 14), a pair only when the pointer is non-nil (a non-nil block is always written, even when all its fields are zero); `Merchant` fields are 1 to 9 in the order of the table of spec 3.2. Source: spec-config 8.2.2 ("the next free id, in the pull request that adds it").
- **D516** The merchant role joins `five`/`two` only through the overlay, as roles with no recipe and weight 1, and no warm-start group; the warm start of the base pack is not changed. Whether the solver accepts a no-recipe role in a pack is open (reference-chain 16 item 2): Phase 8 probes it and reports (section 9 item 6).
- **D517** Test seams: the contract round is proven in package `economysim` with a hand-written scripted strategy (a fake at an extension point, testing 6.1) because `strategy/` imports the root and an internal test cannot import it; the merchant end to end is proven in `package economysim_test` and in `strategy/`.
- **D518** Scenarios assert only exact checks (conservation, `WithAudit`, bit-identical resume, refusal counts, the table of 9.7); the banded checks of X1, S67, S69, S70 report their measured value and do not assert, because no band is set until a reviewed run sets it (reference-chain 7.9, FR-REF-023, FR-REF-038).
