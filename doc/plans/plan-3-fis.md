## Decisions

D1. **Hash id 20 and the Go order.** `Modules.Fiscal` is the 20th pointer of the row. Its Go line is appended after the last pointer that exists (`Tenure` or `Technology`, whichever is last in `Modules` at implementation), in `Modules`, in `canonical` and in `modulesOn`; its pair goes into `pairsLastModules` between id 19 (Polity) and id 24 (Technology), and the comment of that function ("ids 15, 19, 24 and 25") is updated. The inner ids are declaration order (`spec-config.md` 8.2.2; the row for FIS is added in Phase 1 item 1.10 and pinned by `TestFiscalHashEncodingPinned`): `Fiscal` `Taxes` 1, `Duties` 2, `Tolls` 3, `Payouts` 4, `Purchases` 5, `Releases` 6; `Window` `Start` 1, `Until` 2, `Seasons` 3; each flow `ID` 1, `Authority` 2, then its own fields in declaration order, `Label` having no id. `FlowTarget` gains `Jurisdiction` as its id 5 beside `Kind` 1, `Region` 2, `Role` 3, `Store` 4; `pairsMoneyFlow` writes it (empty writes nothing, so the money-supply hash does not move). Seasons are hashed in the calendar's order (FR-FIS-005). A scale is not hashed (FR-FIS-005). Blocks are written when non-nil; an empty non-nil `Fiscal` differs from nil.
D2. **Four positions, no draw.** P1.7b runs after `p17aSettle` (CRD) and before `p18Walk`; P3.4a runs after `p34Estates` and before `p35Orders`; P3.6c after P3.6a; P4.1a after `p41Clear` and before `p41bPayroll`. The toll is a step inside the dispatch of P3.2(c) and P3.3, not a position. `random.go` and the stream are untouched; the number of words of a tick depends on the configuration and the state only through one tie-break key per treasury order at P4.1 (FR-FIS-054, FIS 10.2.4), as for any order. Every tie is `ordering.Key(t, id)` then the ID.
D3. **No per-agent state, no removal hook, no agent-lifecycle hook** (N-128, FIS 17). The carries are module state: per flow, a slice of `(account ID, carry)` pairs in ascending account ID, for every open account the flow has acted on, zeros included. A removal needs no call: the step of a flow walks its class's accounts in ascending ID against that slice in one merge, and drops a pair whose account is closed as it goes; the save skips closed accounts. A test pins that a removed household's carry is neither saved nor kept (`TestFiscal_ClosedAccountCarryDropped`). `relocationRefusal` gets no row (N-090: a tax keeps no agent away or committed) and a test pins it (`TestFiscal_NoRelocationRefusal`, and `TestFiscal_RemovalLeavesNoCall` for `removeAgent`).
D4. **Class membership is read at the position of the flow** (3.2.1), so a household that changes role or region moves between classes with its carry; the class is resolved per tick from the ledger's agent accounts in ascending account ID, with no cached list that a role change could stale.
D5. **Fiscal reasons are Internal per-flow ledger reasons.** A tax, a duty, a toll and a payout each is a reason named by the flow's ID, kind internal, money (or goods for an in-kind payout); a purchase and a release use `trade` (FIS 3.4.2). The ledger already numbers money-flow reasons as `FlowReason(i)`; the plan adds a second family appended after them (a fiscal flow `j` is `FlowReason(len(money flows) + j)`, canonical order taxes, duties, tolls, payouts), with an Internal row whose accounts are the Agent, Estate and Treasury kinds (a tax: payer kinds to Treasury; a payout: Treasury to payer kinds). The bound `math.MaxUint8 - NumReasons` (`ledger.go:193`) covers 64 + 64. A boundary account is created for none (an internal reason has none, FR-FIS-002), so no account ID shifts. The checkpoint of the ledger saves a flow reason by position (`checkpoint.go:64`); a test pins the fiscal positions and that the format-1 fixtures do not move. Row shape (decided here, tested in Phase 3): the family has two variants, money-only for a tax, a duty, a toll and a money payout, and goods for an in-kind payout (`Config.Fiscal` carries a kind per flow, not a bare `Class`); `AppendFlows`, `BoundaryFlow` and the by-position flow reason of the checkpoint treat an Internal flow as a reason with totals and no boundary row. The Go side is `fiscalReasons(cfg)` in `fiscal.go`, one branch in `ledger.go` `newLedger` (hook class (d) and the ledger-row hook of architecture.md 2.10.1), and the root constant for each reason is its flow ID (`ledgerReason` is for the built-ins).
D5a. **Shared numbers (supervisor allocation of 2026-11-18).** Notes N-389 to N-396 (four are used above); ledger reason codes 52 to 55 are reserved for FIS, but the fiscal reasons are per-flow dynamic reasons numbered after the money flows (D5), so the plan takes no fixed code unless Phase 3 finds a fixed row is needed, and then takes 52 to 55 only; checkpoint tag 30 (EMP has 24); hash id 20; scenarios S132 to S136 (EMP has S97 to S101). None equals a merged or another plan's number. Phase 1 greps again before use.
D6. **A flow at scale 0 is skipped whole** (FIS 13.2), before its base, its due or its order is built, whatever its kind: no carry changes, no event, no count. The scale lives in the module state (per flow, 10 000 by default) and `SetFlowScales` resolves an ID against the money flows first and the fiscal flows second (one namespace, FR-FIS-004), applying atomically as today; `MoneyFlowScaled` is emitted for a fiscal flow with its ID.
D7. **A payer that cannot pay is forgiven, not carried** (FIS 5.4): the carry advances by the due, the collection is `min(due, avail)`, the shortfall is counted in `Short`. The treasury never goes negative: the payouts of one authority and one resource are taken together and cut pro rata with the largest remainder and the key `K(t, owner agent ID)` (FIS 8.4). The cut and the headroom cut are `internal/fiscal` functions with a total order.
D8. **The aggregate events are built from counters in slots the world preallocates** (observability-and-logging.md 4.5): one slot per (flow, region) in canonical order, cleared at P8.6 with the other per-tick counters; no event is constructed without an observer (`w.obs.supplied`); the toll counters are filled at dispatch and flushed at P3.6c.
D9. **Rule constants beside their code** (architecture.md 9.6): the order of the passes of P1.7b, the rounding rules (truncate and carry, except the toll's single truncation, the limit's truncation and clamp, the cut's largest remainder), the key and the order of the cut, the pair rule of 9.4.1, the kind and class constants of the events (`tax`, `duty`, `toll`, `payout`; `households`, `pool`, `treasury`), and the refusal reason `toll_unaffordable`. No default macro constant is added (N-027): a module with no flows does nothing.
D10. **Fixtures.** `overlay/fiscal.toml` and `overlay/fiscal-governed.toml` are the REF overlays (REF 4.5: layered after `overlay/polity.toml` and `overlay/polity-governed.toml`): a head tax, a duty and a payout on `five`, and on `three` with `overlay/trade-three.toml` a toll on a border. They are used by S132 to S136 and the golden. Placeholders are labelled as such (Q10).
D11. **Existing goldens do not move.** `testdata/golden/off/` and the pinned hash stay unchanged at every phase (N-217); a diff there is a failure, not a golden to regenerate. `testdata/golden/fiscal/` is new, recorded once. `golden/all/` and the all-on checkpoint fixture are not touched. No existing golden is regenerated by this row, including `golden/polity/`: the polity overlay is unchanged and the module is off in it.
D12. **Coverage row.** `internal/fiscal 90`, added in Phase 2 with the package (`coverage-check.sh` fails a package with no row; `selftest.sh` is run).
D13. **Sentinels** are root sentinels matched with `errors.Is` (N-075): new `ErrNotGoverned`, `ErrNotCovered`, `ErrFiscalShape`; reused `ErrModuleDependency`, `ErrTooMany`, `ErrInvalidID`, `ErrDuplicateID`, `ErrReasonCollision`, `ErrUnknownJurisdiction`, `ErrUnknownRole`, `ErrUnknownRegion`, `ErrUnknownCommodity`, `ErrUnknownMode`, `ErrUnknownSeason`, `ErrOutOfBounds`, `ErrUnknownFlow`, `ErrModuleOff`. Nothing at run time returns an error from this module (no new call); a ledger refusal in a step is a violated invariant and uses the `must` pattern of `p17Flows`.
D14. **New event types may carry typed IDs** (`JurisdictionID`, `RegionID`, `CommodityID`) because the "never a typed ID" rule of architecture.md 2.10.5 binds fields appended to existing rows. The two appended fields are not IDs: `ShipmentDispatched.Toll` is a `Money` amount and `MarketRow.HeldByTreasury` a `Quantity` (Q9).
D15. **The positions split.** `positionsTrade` is at CCN 9 on the tree (measure first: Phase 4 records the figure); the three new guarded calls (P3.4a, P3.6c, P4.1a) would make 12. Phase 8 splits it once, the established way (plan 2-14 O7, plan 3-crd hook 1): a new `positionsClear` holds P3.5 to P4.1a in source order and `positionsTrade` ends at P3.4a; the comments and the two lists of `tick_internal_test.go` name it. P1.7b joins `positionsFlows` (CCN 4 today, 5 after). Q2.
